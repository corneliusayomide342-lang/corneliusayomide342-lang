### **1. Hotel Booking Analysis (Power BI)**
[🔗 View Project Repository](https://github.com/corneliusayomide342-lang/DTLM-hotel-booking)

- **Brief Description:**
An analysis of 119K hotel bookings for DTLM Hostels, focusing on booking patterns, cancellations, distribution channels and revenue.

- **Tools & Skills Used:**
Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Excel, Data Cleaning, Dashboard Design, Data Storytelling, GitHub Documentation.

- **Project Goals:**
To understand why 37% of bookings are cancelled and which channels, countries and customer types drive revenue, and to give recommendations for pricing and operations.

- **Results:**
Built a 3-page interactive dashboard (CEO, Revenue Manager and Operations Manager). Found that cancellations rise with lead time (18.6% for 0–30 days, up to 67.7% for 366+ days), and that online travel agents bring the most revenue (17.3M) but also cancel the most (43.2%).
